04 / APPROACH

How I Approach Technical Challenges.

Good technical decisions require more than implementation. They require context, investigation, evaluation, and continuous improvement.

STAGE 01

UNDERSTAND

Identify the actual problem, understand the requirements, examine the surrounding context, and recognize technical limitations before choosing a solution.

STAGE 02

RESEARCH

Study documentation, investigate system behavior, compare possible approaches, and develop a clearer understanding of the technical problem.

STAGE 03

BUILD

Translate the selected approach into a functional implementation with organized structure, readable code, and attention to practical requirements.

STAGE 04

EVALUATE

Review functionality, test expected behavior, identify weaknesses, debug problems, and evaluate reliability and security considerations.

STAGE 05

REFINE

Document useful findings, improve the implementation, reconsider previous decisions, and use the experience to improve future work.
